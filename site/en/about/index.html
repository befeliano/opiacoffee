Opia Coffee Co.

About

A coffee shop in Yenibağlar. Short, simple and honest: good beans, careful brewing and a warm table.

A cup, a pause.

Opia Coffee Co. has been on Eti Street in the Yenibağlar neighbourhood of Tepebaşı, Eskişehir since 2019. Our motto is “Awaken the senses”, and the idea is simple: a cup you can drink at your own pace, away from the rush of the city.

Next to the espresso classics our bar serves filter coffee, Red Eye and Black Eye, Turkish coffee and a dedicated Matcha Bar. Whether you love coffee or are just getting to know it, you should find something for yourself.

Our counter has different desserts every day: cheesecake, tiramisu, brownie, tarts and more. Part of the menu changes with the seasons; in winter we serve Golden Latte, Cafe Miel, Chai Tea Latte and salep.

How we see coffee

  1. 01

    Specialty beans

    We work with carefully selected, freshly roasted beans. Ask about the filter coffee of the day and we will tell you its story.

  2. 02

    Careful brewing

    Water, grind, time and temperature are measured for every cup. We aim for a consistent cup, not a show.

  3. 03

    Transparency

    Every item on our menu lists its allergens and nutrition values. You have the right to know what you drink.

  4. 04

    No rush

    Open every day from 09:00 until midnight. For a coffee between classes, after a walk or late in the evening.

Where are we? Yenibağlar.

We are right next to Anadolu University’s Yunusemre Campus and the Vecihi Hürkuş Aviation Park (the Aviation Museum). The city centre, Eskişehir Train Station and Cassaba Modern are a short ride away.
