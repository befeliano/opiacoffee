Cookie Policy

We only use the cookies and browser storage this site needs to work. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What is a cookie?

Cookies are small text files saved on your device when you visit a website. Browser storage (localStorage, sessionStorage) can be used for the same purpose; this policy covers all of them.

What we use

NameTypePurposeDuration

We currently use no statistics, advertising or tracking cookies. If we add any in the future, they will only run with your explicit consent.

Consent

Essential cookies and storage are needed for the site and QR ordering to work. Statistics and third-party content only run with your consent; declining does not affect your use of the site.
