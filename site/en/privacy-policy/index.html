Privacy Policy

Short and clear: we do not collect data that identifies you, we take no payments online and we share no data with anyone.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Scope

This policy covers the Opia Coffee Co. website at https://opiacoffeeco.com and the ordering system used through the QR codes on our tables. Legal details are in our Personal Data Notice.

  • Data controller: Opia Coffee Co.
  • Address: Yenibağlar Mah. Eti Cd. No:41/C, 26170 Tepebaşı / Eskişehir, Türkiye
  • Website: https://opiacoffeeco.com

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, email or address.
  • There is no account, login or contact form.
  • There is no online payment; card details are never entered on our site. You pay at the café, at the counter or at your table.
  • We do not request location, camera or contacts access.
  • We do not store IP addresses with order records.

What a QR order records

Only what is needed to prepare your order: table number, items, options (milk choice, hot or iced and so on), your optional note, the time and the order status. Notes are deleted at the end of the day. The rest (items, quantities, amounts, times) does not identify you and is only used for sales statistics.

Stored in your browser

Your basket and order numbers are stored only in your own browser so they survive a page refresh and you can see your order status. You can remove them at any time by clearing your browser data.

Third parties

  • Hosting provider: may keep access logs for technical and legal reasons.
  • OpenStreetMap: the map only loads if you press “Load map”.
  • Google Maps / Instagram: when you follow these links, their own policies apply.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising or share it with other companies.

Security

The ordering system only works with the codes on our tables; only authorised staff can sign in to the admin panel with a password. Failed admin sign-in attempts are kept as an encrypted digest of the IP address for 15 minutes to block password guessing, then deleted.

Children

Our website does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when needed; the current version is always on this page.
